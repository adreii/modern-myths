# EP01: Everyone Has a Number 🔢 (shot list)

Format: 9:16, 1080x1920, 60 fps, about 62 s of picture before the voice retime, 18 shots, plus a 2 s end card.
Voice: first-person narrator, calm and slightly wry (see `ep01-narration-script.md`). Captions: one word at a time, white with a black stroke, lower third. Numbers in the captions are highlighted yellow.
Filmed in the town (`town-plan.md`): the square enclosed by shops, apartments and the Town Hall, the bakery street, and the Hero's house interior.

## Visual plan

**Contrast and affinity** (Bruce Block). Three components change across the five beats:

| Component | Hook + myth (01–08) | Escalation (09–12) | Turn (13–15) | Landing (16–18) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Space | deep and open: the whole square, the street | flatter and crowded: orbits inside the crowd, then a 135 mm frame that presses everything flat | empty: bare doorsteps, a window, a line of numbers saying nothing | deep and open again: one crane up to the whole town |
| Tone / time | dawn and day | day → dusk | pre-dawn quiet, dawn light, no bakery glow | dawn, then every window warm |
| Colour | yellow numbers everywhere | yellow loudest (the count-up, 10,000 pulsing) | yellow stacked into noise, bakery window dark | warm window light outshines the yellow |
| Movement | gentle crane, slow pans | orbits, crash zoom, handheld shake | static | static, then one slow big move |

**Power flip.**
- **The influencer** is shot from below on a wide lens, as the centre of his world (07).
- **The Hero:**
  - High angle when he's "212" (03).
  - Low-angle orbit at his peak (11).
  - Tiny and alone on a 135 mm lens (12).
  - Eye level with the Baker at the hand-off (16) and for the final close-up (17).

**Echo.** Shots 06 and 13 use the identical framing down the row of doorsteps: full of bread, then bare.

**Motif.** The floating number. It glows into view (01), counts up (11), stacks into noise (15), is held still until it means nothing (17), and ends small under warm windows (18).

**Lens key (Roblox FOV, vertical):** 24 mm = 70–74 · 35 mm = 54 · 50 mm = 40 · 85 mm = 24 · 135 mm = 15.

**End card** (2 s, black): "What's something good you did that nobody saw?" Yellow text, then the title.

## Built and ready in Studio
- **Town:** enclosed square, bakery street, Hero's house (bedroom used in 10). The `Sets.cutaway` helper opens the house for shot 10.
- **Cast:** Hero ("212" → "10,000"), Baker ("9"), Influencer ("50,000"), Kid ("57"), 7 crowd members, all with placeholder acting from `StoryKit/Puppet`.
- **Props:** bread loaves, basket, bread bag, phone; the Baker's bedroom set; 26 floating numbers over the town for 18.
- **Lighting:** `LightingPresets` (pre_dawn_dark, dawn, day, dusk), a fake depth of field for the 85 and 135 mm shots, and warm windows for 18.
- **Controls in Play:** P plays all 18 shots, N/B next/previous, R replays, F returns to free mode.

## Sound
- **Music:** a soft lo-fi piano or music-box loop at about 85 BPM. It drops out at 13 and comes back for 18. Use royalty-free music only and keep the licence.
- **SFX:**
  - a whoosh on the crash zoom (10) and on each number tick (11)
  - a door knock and a paper-bag rustle (16)
  - morning birds in the bakery shots
  - one soft chime in 17

## Notes for the edit
- Shots 06 and 13 must keep the same framing. The echo of full doorsteps, then bare ones, is the emotional turn.
- Keep the Hero's number visible in every shot from 11 on. It's the myth on screen.
- First 3 seconds: no logo, no intro. Open straight on the glowing number.
- The voice is recorded first; shot lengths get retimed to it.
