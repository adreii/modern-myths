# EP02: The Sneakers 👟 (shot list)

Format: 9:16, 1080×1920, about 68 s, 18 shots plus a 3 s end card. Voice first, picture retimed to it (see `ep02-narration-script.md`).
Captions: one word at a time, white with a black stroke, lower third; prices highlighted **mint**.
Filmed in the Old Town's School (`lib/town_layout.yaml`, the shell at x -90, z 110) with the interior from `lib/town.py` (hallway with lockers), its schoolyard, and the Hero's bedroom (`HeroHouse` in `lib/town.py`).

Cast: **Hero** (EP01 avatar, plain blue $12 sneakers), **Max** (cool kid, spotless white-and-gold $300 sneakers, not a villain), **Pia** (quiet helper, old grey sneakers mended with tape, no bubble), **Theo** (EP01's Kid, younger), 6–8 students (EP01 crowd avatars), a cousin's giant sneakers (prop only).

## Visual plan

**Contrast and affinity.** Three components change across the beats:

| Component | Hook + myth (01–06) | Escalation (07–11) | Turn (12–16) | Landing (17–18) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Space | the long, deep hallway, ankle-level | crowded and flat: the hallway packed, 135 mm stacks the bubbles | the open, empty schoolyard; Theo small in negative space | deep again: one crane up from the yard |
| Tone / light | flat, even fluorescent indoors | brighter and harsher, blown-out lockers | overcast after rain, low contrast, wet ground | sun breaking through, warm late afternoon |
| Colour | mint price bubbles everywhere | mint loudest, stacked, pulsing | mint drained to grey; Pia's warm bubbles arrive | warm bubbles (cream/orange) outnumber the mint |
| Sound / movement | squeaks, gentle dolly | clomps, orbit, handheld | silence, static | one slow big move, music back |

**Power flip.**
- **Max:** low angle, slow-motion walk, students parting (04). Eye level at the end, stepping into a puddle to help (18).
- **Pia:** edge of frame, high angle, half cut off (05–06). Low-angle hero insert on her taped sneakers (13).
- **The Hero:** high angle and small at "$12" (03), low-angle orbit in the giant shoes (10), kneeling at eye level with Theo (16).

**Echo.** Shots 02 and 17 share one framing: an ankle-level dolly down the hallway floor. 02 is all price bubbles; 17 has the Hero's muddy shoes saying "Theo's puddle" among them.

**Motif.** The speech bubble. It pops up mint with a price (01), stacks into noise (11), is missing over Pia's shoes (05), goes on shouting over empty shoes (15), and comes back warm, saying a place instead of a price (14, 17, 18).

**Lens key (Blender mm on the 36 mm long side):** 24 mm wide · 35 mm natural · 50 mm eye · 85 mm close · 135 mm compressed.

**End card** (3 s, dark): "Where did your feet take you this week, just to help someone?" Warm text, then the title and *after Roland Barthes, Mythologies (1957)*.

## Build this before recording

Reuse first:
- **Old Town** (`lib/oldtown.py`): the School shell, its yard trees, lamps and details. Built once, filmed in every shot.
- **School interior** (`lib/town.py`, `BuildInteriorsPhase2`: hallway with lockers, cutaway walls, `Mark_School_Lockers`). Ported from Studio; check it still builds in Blender and matches the Old Town shell's position.
- **Hero's bedroom** (`HeroHouse` in `lib/town.py`).
- **Cast:** Hero, Kid (= Theo) and the crowd avatars from EP01 (`lib/imported.py`); faces from `assets/faces/`.
- **Acting** (`lib/acting.py`): `walk_to`, `turn_to`, `pose`, `hold` / `release` (books, sheets, marker), `expression`.
- **Camera** (`lib/cam.py`): push, pan, crane, orbit, snap zoom, shake; the `_rig` / `_lookat` spheres.
- **Bubbles:** build on `lib/numbers.py` `tag()` (it already takes value, size, colour, height, bob).

New builds:
1. **Sneakers on avatars.** Extend `lib/imported.py` to hang accessories on the feet (it does head and body now). Four procedural Roblox-style sneakers: plain blue (Hero), white-and-gold glossy (Max), grey with tape and scuffs (Pia), a giant gold pair at 1.6× (prop and wearable). Plus a shoebox-on-foot variant. No real brand names or logos.
2. **Speech bubble.** A `bubble()` in `lib/numbers.py` (or `lib/bubbles.py`): rounded bubble with a tail, text not just numbers, pop-in with a squash, a squeak cue, stacking into a pile. Two styles: mint price (bold, `$`) and warm handwritten (cream/orange).
3. **Schoolyard.** In `lib/town_layout.yaml` by the School: tarmac, puddles (reuse the fountain's water shader, wet-ground roughness), a bench, a bike rack, a low fence, hopscotch lines.
4. **Two new looks** in `lib/lighting.py`: `indoor_fluorescent` (flat, cool, even) and `overcast_wet` (soft grey sky, wet ground); landing uses `dusk`-warm or a "sun breaking through" variant.
5. **New avatars:** Max and Pia in `assets/roblox/avatars.json` (body colours) and `cast.yaml`.
6. **Acting moves:** kneel, pick up from the ground, step out of shoes, big-shoe clomp walk, slow-motion strut (time-scaled walk), carry a stack of books.
7. **Props:** a stack of books, wet homework sheets, a marker, shoeboxes, a window with a view to the yard.
8. **Pipeline check:** `lib/lighting.py` names the world `EP01_World` and `shots.py` has EP01's look table; generalise anything EP01-specific so a second episode builds cleanly.

## Notes for the edit
- Shots 02 and 17 must keep identical framing and dolly speed: that echo is the emotional landing.
- From 10 on, the Hero's bubble stays visible in every shot it's in until he steps out at 15.
- Keep every bubble in the upper two-thirds of the frame or at ankle height above the caption band.
- First 3 seconds: no logo, no intro. Open straight on the sneaker and its squeak.
