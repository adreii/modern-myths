# Episode slate

Each idea is a Barthes essay, the "In this world" rule, the stake, the twist, and the lesson.

1. **Toys** *(ep01, written)*
   - Rule: In this world, toys play themselves.
   - Stake: Grandpa gives me plain wooden blocks that do nothing.
   - Twist: Bored at night, I build things no factory ever made.
   - Lesson: A toy that does everything makes you a user. One that does nothing makes you a creator.

2. **The World of Wrestling**
   - Rule: In this world, every argument is settled by a wrestling match, and the crowd decides who the villain is before the bell.
   - Stake: My dad gets cast as the villain at the neighbourhood meeting.
   - Twist: I see him lose on purpose so the crowd can feel good.
   - Lesson: Be suspicious of any story that hands you someone to hate.

3. **The Brain of Einstein**
   - Rule: In this world, geniuses keep their brains in a jar on the mantelpiece, and anyone can borrow one.
   - Stake: I borrow a genius brain for my exam.
   - Twist: It only works if I do the homework anyway.
   - Lesson: Genius isn't a magic organ. It's work you can do too.

4. **Soap-powders and Detergents**
   - Rule: In this world, soap ads come true. Foam literally fights dirt like an army.
   - Stake: The foam army declares my muddy dog "the enemy".
   - Twist: The dog was never dirty. It was just a dog.
   - Lesson: Ask what a promise of "purity" is selling you.

5. **Wine and Milk**
   - Rule: In this world, what you drink becomes your personality. Wine drinkers grow mustaches, milk drinkers glow.
   - Stake: To fit in at my uncle's table, I must choose.
   - Twist: Grandma drinks water, and she's the most herself of anyone.
   - Lesson: Some habits are costumes. Choose yours on purpose.

6. **The Face of Garbo**
   - Rule: In this world, when you become famous, your face turns into a mask and you can never take it off.
   - Stake: My sister becomes famous online.
   - Twist: Only the people who knew her before can still see her real face.
   - Lesson: Don't trade your face for an image.

7. **Striptease**
   - Rule: In this world, the more clothes you take off, the more covered you get (feathers, sequins, more feathers).
   - Lesson: Spectacle often hides more than it shows.

8. **The Blue Guide**
   - Rule: In this world, travel guides only show mountains and churches. Cities with people in them are invisible.
   - Stake: I go on holiday and can't see anyone.
   - Twist: I put the guidebook down, and a whole city appears.
   - Lesson: Look at the people, not just the postcard.

9. **The Great Family of Man**
   - Rule: In this world, a photo exhibition makes everyone look the same.
   - Stake: My friend's hunger disappears from the photo.
   - Lesson: "We're all the same" can erase real differences in suffering.

10. **Myth Today** *(series finale or manifesto)*
    - Rule: In this world, anything said three times becomes "natural" and turns to stone.
    - Lesson: Everything "natural" was made by someone. You can remake it.
