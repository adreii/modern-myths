## Description (YouTube Shorts / TikTok / Reels)
In this world, toys play themselves. Kitchens cook, dolls change their own diapers,
and kids just… watch. Then Grandpa gives me a box of plain wooden blocks.

Inspired by Roland Barthes' essay "Toys" (Mythologies, 1957), which argues that toys
that copy adult life turn children into users, while simple blocks let them become creators.

What did you build as a kid? 👇
